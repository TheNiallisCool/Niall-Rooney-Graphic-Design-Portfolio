Rebrand

THE CHALLENGE

Cubic³ was repositioning from a connectivity provider to a global connected mobility technology company. The new positioning needed a visual identity that made complex technology feel accessible and premium.

MY ROLE

I led the visual development of the brand’s new design language across digital, product, campaign and marketing applications, translating the new positioning into a scalable visual language.  

THE APPROACH

The system was built around connectivity, movement and technology. Atmospheric imagery, 3D forms, colour, lighting and graphic elements combined into a language that flexes across products and communications. Then I codified it. Master templates for presentations and documents with embedded brand fonts. A defined type, colour and layout system. A set of AI tools that generate and rebrand material directly against the brand rules, including a review agent that checks output before it reaches a stakeholder. Sales, product and marketing now produce their own collateral. It comes out on brand by default.

THE OUTCOME

Deck and document turnaround down 50%. Full marketing team onboarded to the toolset within three months of rollout. Sales, product and marketing now produce their own collateral, on brand, without design input. Efficiency saving modelled at €250k annually at under 15% company-wide adoption.
